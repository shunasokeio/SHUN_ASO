Starting this blog

Aug 27, 2026

I am starting a small blog to write about research progress, conference takeaways, and things I am figuring out along the way.

Expect posts on federated learning for counseling LLMs, mental-health sensing, and the occasional note from conferences or lab visits.

To add a new post, copy this file in the blog/ folder, update the title and date, and add a line to blog/index.html and the Blog section on the homepage.
